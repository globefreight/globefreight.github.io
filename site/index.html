CUSTOMER HELP

Tracking help center

Find your tracking number, understand common statuses, and know when to contact support.

Tracking help

Have your tracking number and the latest event handy so the team can locate the right shipment.

Open shipment tracking
Contact support
Where is my tracking number?

Look in your shipment confirmation or open the tracking link sent to you. If you received neither, ask the sender for the tracking number.

Why can’t I find my shipment?

Check the number against your confirmation and remove any spaces inside it. If the number still returns no result, ask the sender to verify it and confirm the shipment record is ready.

What does “Label created” mean?

A shipment record has been created. Look for a separate pickup event to confirm that your freight has been collected.

What does “Exception” mean?

An issue was recorded during transit or delivery. Check the latest update for details and contact the sender if you need help.
